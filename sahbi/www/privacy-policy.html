This Privacy Policy explains how Sahbi collects and uses information when you chat on Facebook Messenger. السياسة دي بتوضّح إزاي صاحبي بيستخدم بيانات الشات والسجلات المالية.

1. Who we are / مين إحنا

Sahbi is a personal finance assistant for everyday Egyptian users. The Messenger bot records money operations you send and answers questions from your own books.

  • Channel: Facebook / Meta Messenger
  • Contact: {{ contact_email }}

2. Information we collect / البيانات اللي بنجمعها

DataPurpose
Messenger PSID and Page IDLink your chat to your Sahbi workspace
Message text you sendUnderstand requests and record or query money
Financial records you ask us to saveKeep your income, expenses, sales, purchases, loans, and people
Technical webhook and integration logsSecurity, delivery, and debugging (cleared on a schedule)

We do not ask for bank passwords, card numbers, or national ID. Please do not send those in chat.

3. How we use information / الاستخدام

  • Provide the Sahbi assistant on Messenger and in the Sahbi desk chat
  • Write and read only your workspace books
  • Interpret Egyptian money talk with a local parser and, when needed, an AI model
  • Keep the Messenger webhook reliable

4. Processors / أطراف تانية

  • Meta Platforms — Messenger delivery
  • Hosting / database — store workspace, chat, and ledger records
  • Optional local AI (for example Ollama) — interpret unclear messages; the model does not write the database

5. Sharing / المشاركة

We do not sell your data. We share it only with processors needed to run Sahbi, or if the law requires it.

6. Retention / مدة الاحتفاظ

  • Financial entries stay until you cancel them or ask us to delete your workspace
  • Cancelled entries remain in archive, they are not hard-deleted by chat cancel
  • Webhook and integration logs are cleared on a rolling window (about 30 days)

7. Your choices and deletion / حقوقك ومسح البيانات

You can stop using Messenger at any time. To correct a record, ask Sahbi to cancel or correct it.

Data deletion: email {{ contact_email }} from the same context you use with Sahbi, or message the Page and ask to delete your data. We will unlink your Messenger PSID and remove or anonymize workspace chat and ledger data we control, except records we must keep for a short security period.

If Meta sends a user-data deletion callback, we treat it as a request to unlink and delete that PSID’s Sahbi data.

8. Security / الحماية

Workspaces are separated per user. Meta webhook signatures are verified. No internet transmission is fully secure; keep secrets out of chat.

9. Children / الأطفال

Sahbi is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by Meta in your country).

10. Changes / التغييرات

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change. Continued use means you accept the update.

11. Contact / التواصل

Privacy questions: {{ contact_email }}
